What we thought the model used

IG on spatial inputs only (standard approach)

  • Tbottom dominates: ~40% of |IG|, all years
  • Regime shift: +2.6%/decade (r=0.68, p<0.001)
  • Spatial hotspot: Gulf Stream region
  • Seasonal: Tbot peaks JJA, winds peak DJF

What the model actually uses

Full IG including temporal features (year, month sin/cos):

~50%
year_norm
~42%
month (cos)
<1%
all spatial vars

Spatial IG was computed with temporal context held fixed — the 40% Tbottom was the marginal spatial contribution, not total.

Two-level interpretation

Level 1 · Climatological

Model learned MHW long-term climatology:
more frequent & intense in recent years (trend) and in summer (season).
Valid signal — not a bug.

Level 2 · Physical

Conditional on temporal context, Tbottom is the dominant spatial precursor.
This is the novel finding. Ocean preconditioning increasingly important in a warming climate.
